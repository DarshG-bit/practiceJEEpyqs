# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML5, CSS3, Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+), KaTeX MathML. 100% offline-first, client-side only, zero external build tools, hosted on GitHub Pages.

## Users

JEE (Joint Entrance Examination) Main aspirants preparing for engineering admissions in India. Students practicing high-volume questions under timed exam conditions or reviewing chapter concepts.

## Product Purpose

A focused, high-performance study and testing environment for JEE Main previous year questions (PYQs). Provides chapter-wise active recall practice and authentic Computer-Based Test (CBT) examination simulation according to the National Testing Agency (NTA) pattern.

## Positioning

Unlike bloated commercial EdTech portals with paywalls, trackers, laggy animations, and forced signups, this is an instantaneous, distraction-free, 100% offline-capable, True AMOLED black preparation tool that runs locally or on GitHub Pages.

## Operating Context

- Students studying late at night on OLED / AMOLED laptop and desktop displays in dark environments.
- Timed practice sessions ranging from 15-question rapid sprints (30 min) to full chapter tests (up to 300+ questions).
- Strict exam simulation environment reproducing the official NTA JEE Main CBT interface.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **27 Chapter Banks**: Comprehensive question banks across Mathematics (5 chapters), Physics (14 chapters), and Chemistry (8 chapters).
- **Dual Test Modes**:
  - *Real CBT Exam Mode*: NTA 5-color palette, +4 / −1 negative marking, instant answers hidden, submission confirmation modal, post-exam SVG donut breakdown.
  - *Practice & Learn Mode*: Instant checking, retry mistakes, immediate step-by-step solution viewing.
- **Question Sampling**: Select all questions or random sample presets (15, 25, 30, 50, or custom).
- **True AMOLED Dark Mode**: Strict `#000000` background for 100% OLED diode turn-off with zero navy blue.
- **Client Storage Schema**: Unbreakable `localStorage` persistence across `pyq_*` (active session), `pyqh_*` (attempt history), `pyqs_*` (starred questions), and `pyqw_*` (mistakes bank).

## Brand Commitments

- Utilitarian, academic, and serious. No AI gimmicks, no cartoon emojis, no neon glow halos, no gamified badges.
- Elite typography and layout precision adhering to Impeccable's Operate-mode design standards.

## Evidence on Hand

- 27 curated chapter practice files with embedded base64 scientific diagrams and official NTA question keys.
- Comprehensive subject indexes and chapter type notes.

## Product Principles

1. **The Tool Disappears into the Task**: Zero friction between the student and the question. Fast loading, instant transitions, and familiar native controls.
2. **Honest Exam Realism**: Exactly simulate official NTA scoring (+4/−1), question palette states, and timer urgency so students develop real exam temperament.
3. **Protect the Diode & the Eyes**: Pure `#000000` AMOLED dark mode preserves OLED battery and eliminates eye strain during late-night study sessions.
4. **Never Lose Work**: State is automatically saved on every click; accidental refreshes or closes can be resumed with a single click.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA compliant contrast (≥4.5:1 for body text, ≥3:1 for large text).
- Full keyboard hotkeys navigation (`1–4`, `A–D`, arrows, `M`, `C`, `S`, `F`, `?`).
- Clear focus indicators (`:focus-visible`) and reduced-motion considerations.
