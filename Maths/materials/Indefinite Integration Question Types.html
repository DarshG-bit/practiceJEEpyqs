3. Type-by-type detail

Type 1: Trigonometric integrals – 16 questions

What is asked: Integrate an expression in sin x, cos x or tan x, often with an unknown constant to be found from the answer.

What to master: Substitutions t = tan x, t = sin x ± cos x, t = sin x; identities; half-angle and reduction formulas; partial fractions after the substitution.

Forms that appear: ∫ cos θ/(5 + 7 sin θ − 2cos²θ), ∫ sin(5x/2)/sin(x/2), ∫ (cos x − sin x)/√(8 − sin 2x), ∫ dθ/(cos²θ(tan 2θ + sec 2θ)), ∫ cosec⁵x, ∫ (2 − tan x)/(3 + tan x), ∫ sin x/(sin³x + cos³x).

Asked in: 2019 (4), 2020 (2), 2021 (3), 2022 (1), 2023 (1), 2024 (5)

Which form was asked:

Rational functions of sin x and cos x: substitutions t = tan x, t = sin x ± cos x, t = sin x: 9 questions

Standard trigonometric forms: identities, reduction and sec 2x type roots: 5 questions

Hard trigonometric surds with a parameter θ: 2 questions

Type 2: Integration by parts and product-rule forms – 14 questions

What is asked: Find an antiderivative of the form eˣ·f, g(x)·e^h(x) or a quotient by recognising a derivative, or integrate a standard product by parts.

What to master: ILATE; ∫ eˣ(f + f′) = eˣ f; differentiating the proposed answer; comparing both sides to find f or g.

Forms that appear: ∫ x³ sin x, ∫ sin⁻¹(√x/√(1 + x)), ∫ x⁵e^{−4x³}, ∫ eˣ(x sin⁻¹x/√(1 − x²) + ...), ∫ e^{sin²x}(cos x sin 2x − sin x), ∫ (x/(x sin x + cos x))², ∫ (sec²x − 2022)/sin^{2022}x.

Asked in: 2019 (4), 2020 (3), 2022 (3), 2023 (2), 2025 (2)

Which form was asked:

Integration by parts: standard products: 5 questions

The eˣ[f(x) + f′(x)] and e^g(x) forms: 5 questions

Recognising the derivative of a product or quotient: 4 questions

Type 3: Substitution after dividing by a power of x – 13 questions

What is asked: Integrate an expression with fractional powers of (x − a)/(x + b), or with several powers of x, by a clever substitution.

What to master: u = (x − a)/(x + b) when the exponents add to −2; w = 1 + x⁻ᵏ after taking out the highest power; t = x ± 1/x.

Forms that appear: ∫ dx/((x − 11)^{11/13}(x + 15)^{15/13}), ∫ dx/∜((x − 1)³(x + 2)⁵), ∫ (5x⁸ + 7x⁶)/(x² + 1 + 2x⁷)², ∫ cos x/(sin³x(1 + sin⁶x)^{2/3}), ∫ (x⁸ − x²)/((x¹² + 3x⁶ + 1) tan⁻¹(x³ + 1/x³)).

Asked in: 2019 (4), 2020 (2), 2021 (3), 2024 (2), 2025 (2)

Which form was asked:

Fractional powers of (x − a)/(x + b): 4 questions

Divide by a power of x: w = 1 + x⁻ᵏ and similar substitutions: 7 questions

Integrals using x + 1/x and x − 1/x: 2 questions

Type 4: Algebraic roots – 10 questions

What is asked: Integrate an expression containing √(x² + bx + c), √((x + a)/x) or a linear expression under a root, and often use a given value to fix the constant.

What to master: Substitution u = the root or the expression under it; x = a sin θ for √(a² − x²); matching the answer by differentiating.

Forms that appear: ∫ (√(1 + x² + x))¹⁰/(√(1 + x²) − x)⁹, ∫ (1/x)√((1 − x)/(1 + x)), ∫ (x + 1)/√(2x − 1), ∫ x³√(3 − x²), ∫ √((x + 7)/x), ∫ dx/((3 + 4x²)√(4 − 3x²)).

Asked in: 2019 (2), 2020 (1), 2021 (1), 2022 (1), 2023 (2), 2025 (3)

Which form was asked:

Roots of quadratics and of (x + a)/x: 4 questions

A linear or polynomial expression under a root: 4 questions

Trigonometric substitution for √(a² − x²): 2 questions

Type 5: Rational functions: partial fractions and quadratic denominators – 6 questions

What is asked: Integrate a rational function, or a rational function of eˣ, using partial fractions or the reduction formula for 1/(quadratic)².

What to master: Partial fractions; completing the square; ∫ du/(u² + k²)²; t = eˣ for ∫₀^∞.

Forms that appear: ∫ 2x/((x² + 1)(x² + 3)), ∫ dx/(x² + x + 1)², ∫ dx/(x² − 2x + 10)², ∫ (2x³ − 1)/(x⁴ + x), ∫₀^∞ 6/(e³ˣ + 6e²ˣ + 11eˣ + 6).

Asked in: 2019 (2), 2021 (1), 2023 (2), 2025 (1)

Which form was asked:

Partial fractions: 4 questions

1/(x² + bx + c)²: the reduction formula: 2 questions

Type 6: Exponential and logarithmic forms – 5 questions

What is asked: Integrate ratios of eˣ and e⁻ˣ, powers of eˡⁿ, or expressions containing xˣ and (x/e)ˣ.

What to master: Numerator = A(denominator) + B(denominator)′; e^{n ln x} = xⁿ; derivatives of xˣ and (x/e)ˣ; t = x eˣ.

Forms that appear: ∫ (2eˣ + 3e⁻ˣ)/(4eˣ + 7e⁻ˣ), ∫ (e^{3 ln 2x} + 5e^{2 ln 2x})/(e^{4 ln x} + ...), ∫ ((x/2)ˣ + (2/x)ˣ) ln(ex/2), ∫ ((x/e)^{2x} + (e/x)^{2x}) ln x, ∫ (x + 1)/(x(1 + x eˣ)²).

Asked in: 2021 (2), 2023 (3)

Which form was asked:

Ratios of eˣ and e⁻ˣ, and eˡⁿ simplification: 2 questions

x^x, (x/e)^x and x eˣ forms: 3 questions

5. Suggested study order for students

Start with trigonometric integrals and integration by parts (Types 1 and 2): 30 questions (47%). Learn t = tan x, t = sin x ± cos x and eˣ(f + f′) first.

Then the two substitution types (Types 3 and 4): 23 questions (36%). Practise u = (x − a)/(x + b) and dividing by a power of x.

Then partial fractions and quadratic denominators (Type 5): 6 questions, and exponential and logarithmic forms (Type 6): 5 questions.

6. A routine to follow for every question

Look at the integrand first: a root, a ratio of powers, a trigonometric expression, eˣ or a product of two different functions.

Check whether the numerator is the derivative of something in the denominator (or the root), then try u = that expression.

If the powers of x are large or fractional, divide by the highest power of x and look for d(x⁻ᵏ).

If the answer is given with unknown constants, differentiate the answer and compare coefficients instead of integrating.

Check by differentiating the option you chose; use the options to see which substitution they suggest.
