Types of questions asked in JEE Main 2019–2025 (225 questions)

Key message: these 225 questions fall into only 7 repeating types. The three biggest types (Types 1, 2, 3) account for 150 questions (67%). The two property-based types (Types 1 and 2) alone are 105 questions (47%). About 27% of the questions ask for a numerical (integer) answer, so there are no options to guess from.

How to read this: a “type” is the formula, law or technique that decides the answer. So every type below tells you what the question looks like and which formula or method to use.

3. Type-by-type detail

Type 1: King's rule and symmetry (a + b − x, even/odd, periodic) – 53 questions

What is asked: Evaluate an integral whose integrand looks hard, by using a property of definite integrals instead of finding the antiderivative.

What to master: ∫ₐᵇ f(x)dx = ∫ₐᵇ f(a+b−x)dx; even/odd functions on [−a, a]; the trick for 1/(1 + eˣ); periodic functions.

Forms that appear: ∫₀^π 8x/(4cos²x + sin²x), ∫ cos^2023 x/(sin^2023 x + cos^2023 x), ∫ over [−π/2, π/2] of 1/((1 + eˣ)(sin⁶x + cos⁶x)), ∫ x sin x cos x/(sin⁴x + cos⁴x), ∫ ln(x + √(x²+1)), an integral of f(x)/(1 + 3ˣ).

Asked in: 2019 (4), 2020 (3), 2021 (11), 2022 (6), 2023 (7), 2024 (14), 2025 (8)

Which form was asked:

King's rule: ∫ₐᵇ f(x)dx = ∫ₐᵇ f(a + b − x)dx (including f(x)/(f(x) + f(a + b − x)) and ∫ x f(sin x)): 30 questions

Even and odd functions on symmetric limits, and the 1/(1 + aˣ) trick: 20 questions

Periodic functions and f(x) + f(x + T) conditions: 3 questions

Type 2: Greatest integer, modulus and piecewise integrals – 52 questions

What is asked: Integrate a function containing [x], |x|, {x}, max or min, by splitting the interval at the points where the expression changes.

What to master: Where [g(x)] or |g(x)| changes; breaking the interval; adding the pieces; ∫₀ⁿ g({x}) = n∫₀¹ g.

Forms that appear: ∫₀² (2x² − 3x + [x − 1/2]), ∫₀^π |sin 2x|, ∫₋₂² |3x² − 3x − 6|, ∫₁³ [x² − 2x − 2], ∫₀^{e³} [1/(eˣ − 1)], max{x + 1, ..., x + 5}, Σ∫ e^{x − [x]}.

Asked in: 2019 (4), 2020 (6), 2021 (15), 2022 (11), 2023 (8), 2024 (4), 2025 (4)

Which form was asked:

Type 3: Direct evaluation: substitution, partial fractions, by parts, trigonometric integrals – 45 questions

What is asked: Find the exact value by choosing the right substitution or method (no special property is needed).

What to master: Standard integrals; substitution and its limits; partial fractions; rationalising; tan x and tan(x/2) substitutions; integration by parts.

Forms that appear: ∫ 48/√(9 − 4x²), ∫ dx/((x + α)(x + α + 1)), ∫ 1/(√(3 + x) + √(1 + x)), ∫ 1/(3 + 2 sin x + cos x), ∫ eˣxˣ(2 + ln x), ∫ ln(√(1 − x) + √(1 + x)).

Asked in: 2019 (6), 2020 (5), 2021 (5), 2022 (7), 2023 (10), 2024 (9), 2025 (3)

Type 4: Leibniz rule and integral equations – 35 questions

What is asked: Differentiate an integral with variable limits, find a limit of ∫/x, or find a function from an equation that contains an integral of itself.

Type 5: Limit of a sum (Riemann sums) – 16 questions

What is asked: Evaluate lim Σ as an integral, or find the value of an expression involving such a limit.

What to master: lim (1/n) Σ f(r/n) = ∫₀¹ f(x)dx; choosing f and the limits; dividing the terms by n.

Forms that appear: lim (1/n)(f(0) + f(5/n) + ...), lim n³/((n² + k²)(n² + 3k²)) summed, lim Σ 1/(n + r), lim 1/n Σ (2j − 1 + 8n)/(2j − 1 + 4n), lim Σ n/(n² + r²).

Asked in: 2019 (2), 2021 (6), 2022 (4), 2023 (3), 2024 (1)

Type 6: Reduction formulas, recurrences and the Beta function – 14 questions

What is asked: Find a relation between Iₙ, Iₙ₊₁ and Iₙ₋₁, or use B(m, n) to simplify ∫₀¹ xᵐ⁻¹(1 − x)ⁿ⁻¹dx.

What to master: Integration by parts for Iₙ; substitution of the given n; the Beta function and its identities.

Type 7: Abstract functions, bounds and inequalities – 10 questions

What is asked: Use a given property of f (not its formula) to find an integral, or bound an integral between two numbers.

5. Suggested study order for students

Start with the properties and the special functions (Types 1 and 2): 105 questions (47%). Master the a + b − x rule and splitting at [x] and |x|.

Then direct evaluation, limit of a sum and recurrences (Types 3, 5 and 6): 75 questions (33%).

Then Leibniz rule and integral equations (Type 4): 35 questions (16%), and abstract functions and bounds (Type 7): 10 questions.

6. A routine to follow for every question

Look at the limits and the integrand first: symmetric limits, [x] or |x|, a repeating pattern, a sum with n.

Test the properties in order: even or odd, then a + b − x, then periodic; if none helps, try substitution.

For [x] and |x| list the points where the expression changes and split the interval.

For an integral equation differentiate both sides (Leibniz) and use the value at the lower limit.

Check the answer: the sign of the integrand, symmetry, and that the options differ in the form you obtained.
